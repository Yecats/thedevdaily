# The Dev Daily

## AssetFurnace turns prompts into rigged, animated characters — free and local

**Section:** Unity & Game Dev
**Featured:** true
**News date:** 2026-11-03
**Availability:** Shipped
**Tags:** AI, 3D, Animation, Open Source

**Summary:** Four releases this week made the local, credit-free character studio lighter and sharper: FLUX.2 is now the default image engine and VRAM targets dropped to roughly 8 GB.

![AssetFurnace banner: an idea or a picture in, textured rigged animated 3D out](https://repository-images.githubusercontent.com/1320384572/0b642558-002b-43bf-a0b4-32c1b159c905)
*Image credit: [AssetFurnace repository on GitHub](https://github.com/Bingeljell/image-to-3dlab).*

### About the tool

AssetFurnace (formerly "Image to 3D Lab," a GitHub project by Bingeljell) is a local, credit-free studio that turns a sentence or picture into a textured, auto-rigged, animated game-ready GLB, stacking open models — TRELLIS.2, Pixal3D, Hunyuan3D, SF3D — under one UI on Apple Silicon or NVIDIA.

### What changed

Releases v0.5.4 through v0.5.7 shipped between October 31 and November 3 (dates confirmed via the GitHub API): FLUX.2 (Apache-2.0, outputs freely usable) became the default image engine, a back-view second image is accepted for multi-view generation, an inpaint brush redraws only painted spots on the source image, and VRAM was cut hard — about 9.5 GB down to about 4 GB for the background-removal step, and roughly 5 GB total on "FLUX.2 klein Light." Twenty-one motion presets ship built in, and a paid cloud fallback exists at about 20 cents per character.

### Why it matters

This is the most demo-able free Meshy alternative of the week — no credits, license-tracked, runs on an 8 GB card, and covers the generate → rig → animate gap most generators leave open.

### Try or teach

"Zero to animated character for free, locally" is a ready-made tutorial; contrast the output and cost against one Meshy or Tripo credit run.

### Limitations

Single-maintainer project; the quality and VRAM claims are the author's own.

## Tripo launches Agentic Canvas: an AI agent for 3D workflows

**Section:** AI Tools
**Featured:** false
**News date:** 2026-11-02
**Availability:** Beta
**Tags:** AI, 3D, Workflow

**Summary:** An AI agent now plans and runs multi-step 3D pipelines — generate, texture, rig, export — as reusable node graphs, in beta for Max and Team plans.

![Tripo Agentic Canvas announcement showing a cyberpunk scene](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS2025611/Tripo-Agentic-Canvas.jpg)
*Image credit: [Tripo announcement / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tripo-ai-unveils-agentic-canvas-to-make-ai-3d-more-accessible-and-scalable-302926715.html).*

### About the tool

Tripo ([tripo3d.ai](https://www.tripo3d.ai/)) is one of the leading text/image-to-3D generators and a direct Meshy competitor. Agentic Canvas adds an AI agent that plans and runs those workflows as visual node graphs you can save, duplicate, and batch.

### What changed

Announced on the Tripo blog October 29 with a press release November 2, the beta is rolling out through November to Max and Team plans with a free 24-hour trial. The same monthly digest shows a 3D-printing panel now wired into Bambu Studio, Creality Print, OrcaSlicer, and ElegooSlicer, plus a SketchUp bridge.

### Why it matters

This is the first major 3D generator reframing itself from "model button" to "agent pipeline" — a direct answer to where Meshy-style tools are heading, and the slicer integrations push generated assets straight toward print-ready output.

### Try or teach

Run the 24-hour trial on a simple prop and compare the "idea to rigged GLB in one graph" flow against doing the same steps manually in Meshy — great comparative-tutorial material.

### Limitations

Beta availability timing comes from Tripo's own blog; output quality against Meshy or Rodin is untested.

## Idol: one photo to a million-splat 3D model in under two seconds

**Section:** AI Tools
**Featured:** false
**News date:** 2026-10-29
**Availability:** Shipped
**Tags:** AI, 3D, Gaussian Splatting, Open Source

**Summary:** The open-weights Idol model turns one photograph into up to a million Gaussian splats in seconds, and it is trending on Hugging Face.

### About the tool

Idol (published under "Speridlabs" on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/)) generates a 3D Gaussian splat from one photograph — up to about a million splats as standard .ply, with unseen sides hallucinated — in about 7.2 GB of VRAM on consumer GPUs, with a Python SDK and a free Hugging Face Space.

### What changed

Apache-2.0 weights, an SDK, and a hosted demo are all out, with roughly 19k downloads and 800-plus likes within days of the October 29 model-card update — and combined with the Blender news below, splats are clearly the theme of the week.

### Why it matters

Free, fast, local image-to-3D lookdev that drops into engines as splats — great for blocking out real-world props before committing to meshed assets.

### Try or teach

Pop a reference photo of a real prop into the demo Space, export the .ply, and import it as scene dressing — pair it with the Blender 5.3 story for a "splats in the pipeline" segment.

### Limitations

Outputs splats, not textured PBR meshes — lookdev and preview territory, not final assets; the lab behind it could not be independently confirmed.

### Sources

- Hugging Face model card
- Project page
- Live demo

## HairDeck: one image to game-ready hair cards with spring bones

**Section:** AI Tools
**Featured:** false
**News date:** 2026-10-27
**Availability:** Shipped
**Tags:** AI, Character Art, Open Source

**Summary:** Research from Tsinghua, CMU, and CUHK converts a single character image into stylized hair cards with per-card UVs and animatable spring-bone chains.

![HairDeck result poster showing rigged hair cards from a single image](https://huggingface.co/hyz317/HairDeck/resolve/main/assets/video-poster.jpg)
*Image credit: [HairDeck model card on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/hyz317/HairDeck).*

### About the tool

HairDeck converts a single character image into stylized hair cards — per-card UVs, colors, and the spring-bone chains that animate them — exported as GLB or a VRM 0.x avatar.

### What changed

A full open release: the Image, Chain, Color, and Point-cloud DiT models plus a Card VAE, a demo Space, and an arXiv paper. The output format — cards plus rig — is unusually game-pipeline-native.

### Why it matters

Stylized hair cards with a working rig are a famously manual, hated chore, and the big generators (Meshy, Tripo) don't touch them — this is exactly the pipeline niche tutorials thrive on.

### Try or teach

Generate hair from one concept image, export the GLB, and compare the card layout and bone chains against a hand-built setup in Blender.

### Limitations

Released October 27, just before the coverage window; local use needs CUDA plus SAM 3 / DINOv3 licenses.

## Blender 5.3 beta gains native Gaussian splatting

**Section:** 3D & Blender
**Featured:** false
**News date:** 2026-10-30
**Availability:** Beta
**Tags:** Blender, 3D, Rendering

**Summary:** The first native Gaussian splat support lets .PLY and .SPZ captures import, edit, and render inside both of Blender's render engines.

![Gaussian splat scene in Blender](https://code.blender.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/gaussian_splat.jpg)
*Image credit: [Blender development blog](https://code.blender.org/2026/10/splatting-the-gaussians-into-blender).*

### About the tool

Blender is the core DCC of most indie asset pipelines; its development blog announced first-class support for Gaussian splats — point clouds with view-dependent (spherical-harmonic) color.

### What changed

Splats in .PLY and .SPZ import, edit, and render in both engines: Cycles uses ray-oriented intersection, while EEVEE projects camera-oriented billboards and needs higher sample counts (dithered transparency gets noisy). Relighting is early-stage with normals estimated from splat scale. Blender 5.3 final is expected around November 10.

### Why it matters

Photogrammetry and splat-captured real-world assets becoming first-class citizens inside Blender is a big pipeline shift — and the 5.3 final release days away is a guaranteed content beat.

### Try or teach

Record the "phone scan → splat → Blender 5.3 beta → cleaned reference mesh" tutorial now so it lands the week 5.3 ships — combine with the Idol story.

### Limitations

5.3 is a beta; editing and relighting tools are explicitly initial-stage.

### Sources

- [Blender development blog](https://code.blender.org/)
- [Blender downloads](https://www.blender.org/download/)

## Rider users: compromised eMudhra certificates require a JetBrains update

**Section:** Other Tools
**Featured:** false
**News date:** 2026-10-30
**Availability:** Shipped
**Tags:** Security, JetBrains, PSA

**Summary:** Compromised certificate keys affect JetBrains IDEs, ReSharper, .NET Tools, Toolbox, and Gateway — every Unity developer on Rider should update now.

### About the tool

JetBrains Rider is a primary C# IDE for Unity developers; its bundled JetBrains Runtime carries its own certificate trust store, separate from Windows and macOS.

### What changed

Private keys for eight eMudhra intermediate certificates were compromised, creating a network-impersonation risk. JetBrains IDEs, ReSharper, .NET Tools, Toolbox App, and Gateway are affected; fixed versions must be installed, and older IDEs (2025.1 and earlier) plus Toolbox below 3.7 require migration. OS updates do not fix it, because the vulnerability lives in the bundled runtime.

### Why it matters

Every Unity developer on Rider needs to act, and a short PSA is exactly the kind of trust-building content that outperforms feature news.

### Try or teach

A 60-second "if you use Rider or Unity via JetBrains tools, update today" short or community post.

### Limitations

The exact publication day (October 30) comes from the feed pubDate; the advisory page shows the month only.

### Sources

- JetBrains security advisory
